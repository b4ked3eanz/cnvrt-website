1  Hit arm + open site above — it opens the site with ?probe=1, which turns the probe on and remembers it.
2  Scroll the whole page by hand at a reading pace. Or press Shift+S and it will sweep itself top to bottom.
3  Come back here and hit reload.

The probe only records frames while the page is actually MOVING, so parking on a section does not dilute it with cheap idle frames. It buckets by 100px of scroll, and it charges every rAF driver, every SY subscriber and the four synchronous stalls (toDataURL, toBlob, getImageData, readPixels) their own SELF time — so an encode shows up as the encode and not as whoever asked for it.

It has to be a real window you are scrolling with your own hand. A hidden tab does not fire rAF and a scripted one is presentation-throttled — see PERF.md → How to measure, which exists because this project has been wrong about exactly that three times.
